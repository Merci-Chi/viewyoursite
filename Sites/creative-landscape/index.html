Why we sketch before we plant

A landscape is a living drawing. It changes with the light, the seasons, and the years. Designing on paper first means fewer surprises and a yard that matures the way you imagined it.

1. Listen

Walk the property together. Note the sun, the soil, the drainage, and the way your family uses the space today.

2. Draw

Turn the walk into a plan: bed shapes, plant groupings, and focal points, sketched to scale so you can see it before it is real.

3. Plant

Install with care for spacing, sunlight, and water, so the garden grows in the direction the sketch pointed it.

Landscape design

Scaled planting plans and bed layouts drawn around your home, your light, and your soil. Typical projects include new beds, full front-yard refreshes, and foundation plantings.

Trees and shrubs
