Salvadoran Bakery & Kitchen · North Las Vegas

A little taste of home, baked fresh every day.

Panaderia Salvadoreña on East Lake Mead Blvd is a bakery and a little kitchen in one: semitas and quesadilla salvadorena by the register, tamales warm and wrapped, pan dulce on the trays, and the kind of welcome that keeps neighbors coming back.

The staples neighbors ask for by name

Semitas, quesadilla salvadorena, tamales, and pan dulce, baked fresh every day for North Las Vegas.

Semitas

Soft, sweet, and layered the traditional way. The first thing regulars look for when the trays come out.

Quesadilla Salvadorena

The rich, cheesy-sweet Salvadoran tea cake. Dense, golden, and perfect with your morning coffee.

Tamales

From the kitchen side of the house, wrapped and ready to take home for the family table.

Pan dulce & bolillos

Trays of sweet bread and crusty bolillos baked through the day, right next to Central American favorites.
