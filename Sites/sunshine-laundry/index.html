Sunshine Laundry services

Two ways to get it clean

Do it yourself or let the counter take it from here.

Self-service washers and dryers

Load up, pick your settings, and let the machines do the work. Switch over to a dryer as soon as the wash is done and you will be home before you know it.

Wash & fold drop-off

Drop your bag at the counter and pick it up washed, dried, and folded. Call the shop and we will walk you through how drop-off works.

Staffed, not self-serve help

An attendant is on site during open hours, so there is always someone to ask about machines, supplies, or a stuck sock.
