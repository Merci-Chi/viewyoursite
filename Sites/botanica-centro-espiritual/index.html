What you will find here

The shelves cover the essentials of a good botanica, and we are happy to help you find the right thing for what you are working on.

Yerbas & herbs

Romerito, ruda, romero and more, sold by the bundle for baths, limpias and teas.

Veladoras

Prepared and plain candles in glass, in the colors and prayers for many intentions.

Oils & waters

Esoteric oils, colognes and Florida water for dressing candles and anointing.

Limpias & cleansings

Spiritual cleansings are part of what a centro espiritual is for. Ask us in store about a limpia.

Saints & statues

Small statues, prints and religious articles for your home altar or grotto.

Incense & copal

Sticks, cones and resins to keep your space clear and your altar smoking.
