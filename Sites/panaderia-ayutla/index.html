Panaderia Ayutla is the family-run panaderia in Rogers Park with trays of pan dulce, soft bolillos, orejas and cookies, baked fresh every morning before the doors open at 6.

What comes out of the oven

Trays refill through the day, but early birds get the warm ones.

Pan Dulce Trays

Conchas, cuernos, puerquitos and the day's sugar-dusted batch, laid out on trays so you can pick piece by piece.

Bolillos & Breads

Crusty bolillos and teleras for the week's tortas, plus soft sandwich loaves the neighbors call the reason they skip the supermarket.

Orejas & Favorites

The palm-pastry orejas that people come back for, layered crisp and buttery, waiting near the register where they never last.

Cookies

Polvorones, sugar cookies and wedding-style cookies by the dozen for office tables and after-dinner plates.

Morning Batch

Doors open at 6 AM with the first bake already cooling. The 7 AM line knows why they came.

Family Counter

A family-run bakery: the same hands that fill the trays wrap the pink paper and ring you up.

Pan de cada día

Every day at 6 in the morning the ovens go on, and by the time the doors open the cases smell like a Mexico City corner bakery. Grab a tray, walk the cases, pick what calls to you, and pay at the counter. That is how a panaderia is supposed to work.

How the tray works

No counter ordering, no waiting. Tong in one hand, tray in the other.

1

Grab a tray

Tongs and trays are stacked right at the start of the cases.

2

Pick your fav items

Walk both walls. Mix pan dulce with bolillos however you like.

3

Pay at the counter

We wrap it in paper, bag it, and you are out the door in minutes.
