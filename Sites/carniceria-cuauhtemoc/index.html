La Carniceria

Three counters, one market. This is what to ask for when you walk in.

The meat counter

Fresh cuts cut to order: steaks for the grill, stew meat, ribs, and the marinated favorites for the weekend.

La tortilleria

Delicious, fresh corn AND flour tortillas made in store. Grab a dozen warm and they rarely make it home unopened.

Produce

Tomatoes, chiles, cebollas, cilantro, limes. The produce section is always well stocked.

Saturday prep, the smart way

Marinated and ready to grill: ask the carnicero what came in this morning. The tortillas come out in batches, so call ahead for big family orders.
