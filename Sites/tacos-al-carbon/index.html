The menu

Charcoal, tortillas, and not much else

Tacos Al Carbon Mexican Food Truck keeps the menu tight so every order comes off the grill fresh made. Here is what the window serves, Thursday through Sunday from 5 PM to midnight.

Tacos al carbon

The classic, done big: your choice of carne asada or lengua, grilled over charcoal and served on two tortillas with melted cheese between them, guacamole piled on top.

The headliner

Carne asada

Chopped off the grill, charred at the edges and juicy in the middle. Order it in a taco, a burrito, or a quesadilla.

Grilled to order

Lengua

Slow-cooked and tender, the one regulars swear by. Get it doubled up in a taco with the melted cheese and guacamole.

Regular favorite

Burritos

Rolled thick with asada or lengua and the works. Dinner-sized, foil-wrapped, and ready to go.

Filling

Quesadillas

Cheese melted on the comal until the edges crisp, folded over your meat of choice. Add guacamole, no extra lecture.

Crispy edges

Guacamole & toppings

Fresh guacamole goes on the tacos, and you can add it to anything else on the menu. The food is always fresh made, window to hand.
