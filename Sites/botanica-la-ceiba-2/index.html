The yerba wall

Rows of dried herbs and yerbas in labeled bins. Common greens stay in stock week to week, and harder-to-find cuttings come through with the seasonal deliveries.

  • Ruda

Velas & the candle case

Veladoras in glass with printed labels, plain pillars, and vigil candles. We keep the glass case restocked so regulars can count on their color and saint being in.

  • Veladoras
  • Vigil lights
  • Pillar candles
  • Holders

Oils, perfumes & remedios

Esoteric oil lines, waters, and perfumes for dressing candles and anointing, plus the traditional remedio bottles our customers ask for when something needs settling.

  • Perfume lines
  • Bath crudas

Statues, crystals & the altar corner
