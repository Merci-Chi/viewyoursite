The work

What we do at the table

Bring the garment, tell us how it should fit, and we take it from there. Most everyday work is ready within a few days.

01

Hems and lengthening

Pants, skirts, dresses and sleeves, finished by hand where it shows.

02

Dress fitting

Taking in, adding straps, reshaping bodices for that made-for-you feel.

03

Suit and shirt service

Trouser and jacket adjustments so suits sit the way they should.

04

Zipper replacement

Jackets, bags, pants and boots, matched in color and weight.

05

Tears, worn seams, pockets and buttons, made strong again.

06

Wedding and quinceanera

Gown and formal alterations with extra fittings until it is right.
