The Wash

How laundry day works here

Self-service machines

Washers and dryers ready whenever you are. The shop is open 24 hours, so midnight laundry runs are completely normal here.

Wash cards, not quarter hunts

The shop runs on a wash card. Picking one up is cheap, reloading is flexible, and reviews mention the alternatives they allow for reloading.

Wifi while you wait

Free wifi in the shop. Answer emails, stream an episode, or scroll while the dryer does its thing.

People at the ready

Reviewers keep calling out the friendly, helpful staff. Got a question about a machine or your card? Ask away.
