What comes out of the oven

Trays refill through the day

Trays refill through the day, but early birds get the warm ones.

Pan Dulce Trays

Conchas, cuernos, puerquitos and the day's sugar-dusted batch, laid out on trays so you can pick piece by piece.

Bolillos & Breads

Crusty bolillos and teleras for the week's tortas, plus soft sandwich loaves the neighbors call the reason they skip the supermarket.

Orejas & Favorites

The palm-pastry orejas that people come back for, layered crisp and buttery, waiting near the register where they never last.

Cookies

Polvorones, sugar cookies and wedding-style cookies by the dozen for office tables and after-dinner plates.
