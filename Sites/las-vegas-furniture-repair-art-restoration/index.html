The Workshop

Repair is the respectful option

Most furniture was built better than what replaces it. Our work keeps good pieces in living rooms instead of landfills.

i

Furniture repair

Loose joints, broken legs, split tops and sagging frames made solid again.

ii

Refinishing

Old finishes carefully stripped, sanded and renewed until the piece reads like itself again.

iii

Art restoration

Framed and decorative works treated with patience and a light hand.

The Craft

What we take on

Bring the piece by the workshop, or ask us to come to you for larger or fragile items.

i

Furniture repair

Chairs, tables, dressers and cabinets: structural repairs done so the piece is useful, not just presentable.

ii

Refinishing

Water rings, sun damage and tired varnish removed and replaced with a finish suited to how the piece is actually used.

iii

Antique restoration

Old pieces handled with restraint. Repairs kept honest, original character respected, nothing over-restored.

iv

Art restoration

Paintings, frames and decorative pieces cleaned, stabilized and brought back from the brink.

v

On-site assessments

For big, heavy or fragile pieces, we assess the work at your home or office and quote from there.

How a restoration begins

  1. You call, text or send the form below with a description and a photo if you have one.
  2. We assess the piece, here in the workshop or on site.
  3. You get a clear quote, and only then does the work begin.
