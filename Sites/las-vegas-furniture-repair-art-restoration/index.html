The workshop

Repair is the respectful option.

The business focuses on repairing and restoring furniture and art rather than replacing pieces that can be brought back into useful condition.

The available service information includes structural furniture repair, refinishing, antique restoration, art restoration, and on-site assessments.

Services

What we take on

Only services supported by the available business information are shown below.

i

Furniture repair

Chairs, tables, dressers, and cabinets, including structural repairs such as loose joints, broken legs, split tops, and sagging frames.

ii

Refinishing

Refinishing work for worn finishes, water rings, sun damage, tired varnish, and related surface deterioration.

iii

Antique restoration

Restoration work intended to preserve the character of older furniture while repairing damage and wear.

iv

Art restoration

Cleaning, stabilization, and restoration work for paintings, frames, and decorative pieces.

v

On-site assessments

Assessment at a home or office for larger, heavier, or fragile pieces that may not be practical to bring to the workshop.
