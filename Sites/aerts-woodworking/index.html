What the shop does

Services

The shop’s public listing describes the trade as fine woodworking and cabinetry. Specific offerings below reflect that listing; call to confirm your project is a fit. The listing states the shop does not offer free project estimates.

Custom Cabinets

New cabinetry for kitchens, baths, laundry rooms, and home offices. Designed, built, and installed by the owner.

Cabinet Repair

Door and drawer repair, hinge replacement, refacing, and finish touch ups on existing cabinetry.

Built Ins and Shelving

Bookcases, entertainment centers, and storage walls fitted to your exact space.

Furniture Repair

Regluing, refinishing, and structural repair for wood furniture worth saving.

Finish Carpentry

Trim, molding, and detail work finished with a cabinetmaker’s eye.

Custom Pieces

One of a kind tables, boxes, and gifts built from solid hardwood.

How pricing works

Flat rate, direct terms.

Per the shop’s public profile: owner operated with no subcontractors, flat rate charges, no travel charges, and no service fees. Estimates are not free. Every project is discussed directly with the craftsman, so what you are quoted is what the work costs.
