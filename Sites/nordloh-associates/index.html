Why a survey matters

Guessing costs more than measuring.

Buying or selling

Lenders and title companies often want the boundaries confirmed before money changes hands. A survey gives every party the same drawing to work from.

Fences and disputes

A few inches of fence in the wrong place can become a long argument. A surveyor sets the line with instruments, not opinions.

Building and adding on

Setbacks, easements, and lot coverage all start from the true boundary. Measure first, pour concrete second.
