About the group

Greiwe Development Group is a real estate development firm based at 9355 Holly Hill, Cincinnati, OH 45243, led by principal Rick Greiwe.

The firm specializes in building luxury residences in prime locations with exceptional architecture and high quality interior finishes, focused on walkable neighborhoods with a mix of dining, shopping and recreational amenities.

2012

Rick Greiwe of Greiwe Development Group appears before the Mariemont Planning Commission regarding roadway planning tied to development work.

2014

Greiwe Development and North American Properties break ground on 2770 Observatory, a $35 million, 30-unit luxury condominium building in Hyde Park.

2015

Gateway Partners LLC, including Greiwe Development Group, is selected to develop the 12-acre Gateway Redevelopment Area in Montgomery, Ohio.

Sources: UrbanCincy (May 2014; Nov 2015), City of Montgomery 2015 Annual Report, Mariemont Planning Commission minutes (Apr 18, 2012).
