La Carniceria & Taqueria

From the butcher block to the plancha

One shop, two counters. Here is how the day flows at La Hacienda.

Carniceria

Marinades mixed in-house, cuts trimmed to order.

  • Carne asada, marinated and grill-ready
  • Steaks, chops and roasts cut to your pan
  • Ground beef, fresh from the block
  • Marinated pollo and pork for the week
  • Sold by the pound or by the tray for a crowd

Taqueria

The hot counter at the back of the shop.

  • Tacos made to order at the counter
  • Meats straight from our own carniceria case
  • Doors open at 6:30 AM for the early shift
  • Grab-and-go for the McKinley Ave commute
  • The same meats you buy by the pound

The piñata shelf is real

People drive past three other shops for this one. Piñatas in season, ice cream in the freezer, and the taqueria still open at 8:45 PM if the party runs long.
