On the shelves

What the shop carries

A botanica in the classic sense: shelves of practical spiritual goods, chosen and stocked by people who know what each one is for.

Herbs & yerbas

Dried and fresh herbs for baths, sweepings, and simmering pots.

Velas

Candles by color and intention, from small tapers to seven-day glass.

Remedios

Traditional remedies and preparations kept the old way.

Oils & perfumes

Spiritual oils and waters for dressing candles and cleansings.

Crystals & stones

Pieces for the pocket, the altar, or the windowsill.

Statues & figures

Statues and images to set a calm, cared-for space.

La Botanica

Franklin Blvd, Ste B

Botanica Celeste keeps its doors open Tuesday through Saturday on Franklin Blvd. Inside it is all the usual comfort of a working botanica: herbs and yerbas, velas for every intention, remedios, oils, soaps, statues, and crystals, with someone at the counter who can tell you which shelf to start on.
