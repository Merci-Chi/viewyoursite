The concept

Hot pot built around choice.

Public reporting from operator Wing Law describes a “true All-You-Can-Eat” model with food brought out for guests to pick and choose, individual hot-pot setups, genuine bone broth instead of powdered soup base, and a patio designed as a relaxed backyard-style hangout.

All-You-Can-Eat Hot Pot

Chinese hot pot with a rotating selection of meats, seafood, vegetables, noodles and toppings.

Individual Pots

Public reviews and listings describe individual hot pots, making it easier for guests to choose their preferred broth and spice level.

Outdoor Patio

The patio is a central part of the restaurant concept and was highlighted by the operator before opening.
