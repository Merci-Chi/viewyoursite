About the company

Craftsmanship focused on concrete and masonry.

Impressive Creations Inc. is a Greater Cincinnati concrete and masonry contractor. Public business records show the company began in 1999. Its documented service focus includes decorative concrete, concrete repair and restoration, and masonry restoration.

The company has also previously operated under the name Creative Concrete & Masonry.
