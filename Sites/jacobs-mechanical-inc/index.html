FAQ

What public records support.

Does Jacobs Mechanical handle residential HVAC?

Yes. Angi describes a residential division, and public permits show residential furnace, A/C, heat-pump, coil, and air-handler work.

Does Jacobs Mechanical handle commercial HVAC?

Yes. BBB specifically lists commercial heating and air-conditioning services, and public permit records document substantial commercial work.

Does the company fabricate ductwork and sheet metal?

Yes. MacRae's lists ventilation, ductwork, and sheet-metal fabrication, while Angi describes an in-house fabrication shop.

Does Jacobs Mechanical offer emergency service?

Angi advertises emergency services, and public reviews describe same-day response. Exact availability should be confirmed with the company.

How long has Jacobs Mechanical been operating?

The company has roots dating to 1922 under the earlier B&J Jacobs Company name. BBB reports the current corporation started in 1984 and incorporated in 1985.

Is Jacobs Mechanical licensed?

Yes. BBB lists Ohio HVAC license HV.27903, and BuildZoom shows an active Cincinnati license CBRC000538.
