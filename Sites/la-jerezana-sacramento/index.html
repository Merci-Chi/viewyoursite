La Panaderia

What the shop is

La Jerezana is a working Mexican bakery on Rio Linda Blvd, Suite 300. No gimmicks, just trays of pan dulce baked through the day, bolillos for the table, and a counter that will help you build a box for the family.

First time? Tell the staff what you like, sweet, fluffy, fruity, and they will hand-pick a dozen with you.
