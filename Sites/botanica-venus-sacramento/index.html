La Botanica

The shop on Northgate

Botanica Venus sits on Northgate Blvd, a small shop that regulars describe as the one that finally felt right. The shelves carry the herbs, velas, remedios, oils, soaps, statues, and crystals a home altar or a quiet ritual calls for, and the people behind the counter are known for making visitors feel safe and welcomed.

If you are new to botanicas, say so. They are very knowledgeable and happy to start from the beginning, no pressure and no judgment.

Plan your visit

Readings are offered, and appointments are recommended to avoid a wait. Doors open 11 to 5, Monday through Saturday.
