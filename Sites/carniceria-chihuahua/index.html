From the case

Everything behind the glass is cut and handled in house. Tell the counter what you are cooking and they will point you right.

Fresh cuts

Beef, pork and chicken cut at the counter. Ask for the thickness and the portion you want and they will cut it while you wait.

Marinated meats

Seasoned and marinated favorites ready for the grill or the comal, the kind of prep that makes weeknight dinner easy.

The veal chop

A regular's favorite: people come in specifically for the veal chop. Call ahead and ask the counter what is in the case today.

A counter that helps

Friendly, knowledgeable and helpful. If you are not sure which cut to grab, say so and they will walk you through it.

La Carniceria

Cut to order

Milanesas, steaks for the grill, stew meat, the Sunday roast. Say the word and the counter cuts it the way your recipe needs it.

Marinated & preparada
