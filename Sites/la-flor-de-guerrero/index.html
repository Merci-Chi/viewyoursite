The sweet case

Grab one of the bakery trays at the counter and walk the case. It is the best way to shop a panaderia, and the trays fill up fast on weekends.

What you will find

A Mexican bakery counter for everyday bread and every occasion.

Sweet breads

Conchas in classic colors, pig cookies, cuernos, orejas and more, set out in waves through the day.

Everyday bread

Bolillos and teleras with crackly crust, the base for tortas, molletes and a proper bowl of pozole on Sunday.

Cakes and treats

Sweet classics from the case for birthdays, quinces and Sundays at the abuela's table. Ask what is available today.
