On the shelves

Everything an upholstery job needs, under one roof.

If a chair, booth, boat bench, or car seat can be recovered, the supplies to do it are on our shelves. Ask at the counter and we will point you to the right aisle.

Upholstery fabric

Velvets, tweeds, canvas, prints, and solids, sold by the yard off the bolt.

Vinyl & marine vinyl

Automotive grade vinyl built for seats, restaurant booths, and boat interiors.

Foam & padding

Seat foam in several densities, batting, and dacron wrap. We cut to size.

Webbing & springs

Elastic and jute webbing, coil springs, clips, and edge wire for seat rebuilds.

Tools & staples

Staple guns, tack hammers, regulators, ripped chisels, and staples by the box.

Thread & piping

Heavy duty thread, piping cord, welting, zippers, and needle sets.

Buttons & grommets

Covered button kits, grommets, and snap fasteners in common sizes.

Adhesives & care

Spray adhesives, contact cement, and the basics to keep fabric looking new.

How the counter works

Walk in with an idea, walk out with supplies.

Pick your fabric

Browse the bolts at your own pace. Pull a roll down, drape it over your knee, hold it up in the light. Nobody rushes you.

We cut what you need

Tell us the yardage or hand us your old cover as a pattern. We cut foam to size and bundle the small parts you forgot.

Finish the job

Take it home for your own project, or hand the bundle to whoever is doing your sewing. Either way, you bought the right stuff.
