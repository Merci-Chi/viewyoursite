About the studio

A Milford studio with a collector’s eye

Saltbox Home Styling is an interior design business based at 5601 Autumn Wynd Ln in Milford, Ohio. The name nods to the classic saltbox house: practical, unpretentious, and quietly charming. That is the spirit behind the styling here.

Good styling is not about buying everything new. It is about seeing what you have, and arranging it like it matters.
