What a visit holds

Every session is different, because every person is. This is how the work usually unfolds.

Readings

A reading is time set aside for you. You can bring a question about love, family, work, or a decision you keep circling, or you can simply sit and let the reading go where it needs to. Victoria works in the curandera tradition and tells you what she sees, plainly and kindly.

Nothing here is a guarantee, and no honest reader would promise one. What you get is an attentive ear, honest guidance, and something to sit with afterward.

Limpias

A limpia is a traditional spiritual cleansing. With herbs, smoke, and prayer, the heavier stuff gets swept away: the bad week, the bad news, the weight you cannot quite name. People often leave a limpia feeling lighter, quieter, and more like themselves.

Herbs, velas & remedios

The work does not end when you walk out the door. Victoria keeps a small shelf of herbs, candles, and traditional remedios so you can continue the care at home, in your own way and at your own pace.

Take from each session what feels true to you, and leave the rest. That is how this work has always been done.
