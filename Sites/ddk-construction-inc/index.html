About D.D.K. Construction, Inc.

What public records actually show about this Cincinnati general contractor. Nothing below is invented.

The record, in plain terms

D.D.K. Construction, Inc. is a general contractor based at 7259 Dog Trot Rd, Cincinnati, OH 45248. It has been listed in the Blue Book (the construction industry bid and contractor directory) since 1999, under the trade of General Contractors with CSI code 01 35 16 (Alteration Project Procedures).

The company is a member of Allied Construction Industries (ACI), a construction trade association serving the Cincinnati region.

Its listed service area covers Cincinnati, Columbus, and Dayton in southern Ohio. A 2011 Warren County, Ohio bid record shows DDK Construction bidding $128,157.00 on the Zoar Road Bridge Replacement project, consistent with heavy civil and bridge work.
