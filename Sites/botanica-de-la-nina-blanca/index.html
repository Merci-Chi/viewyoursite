The shop, shelf by shelf

An excellent selection of the things you really need – chosen for people who know what they are doing, and explained kindly to those still learning.

i.

Herbs & yerbas

Fresh and dried, sorted and labeled, for baths, burnings, and everything between.

ii.

Candles & veladores

White and colored candles, 7-day glass velas, and votives arranged by intent.

iii.

Oils, perfumes & aguas

Aceites espirituales, colognes, and holy water for cleansings, luck, and protection.

iv.

Soaps & remedios

Jabones and classic remedios for the washes and preparations you already know.

v.

Statues & prints

Santos, framed prints, and altar pieces – clean shelves, fair prices, careful stock.

vi.

Incense & charcoal

For the burnings and the quiet moments that follow them.

Everything on the shelves was chosen for a reason. If you cannot find it, ask – we are thoughtful about this work.
