About

The man behind the work

Beatrice Home Improvement is a one-man handyman operation run by Tim Beatrice out of Milford, Ohio.

Tim Beatrice is a husband, father of five, and grandfather of six. He and his wife Beth live in Clermont County, where they keep a garden and raise chickens.

Veteran of the United States Air Force, having served in the Reserves. Graduate of Miami University, class of 1986.
