La Panaderia

Baked before sunrise

With doors that open at 4:30 AM, the morning bake is the whole show. Come when the trays are still steaming.

The pan dulce case

Conchas, orejas, cuernos and the rotating sweet-bread lineup. Grab a tray, pick your dozen, pay at the counter.

Bolillos & breads

Crisp bolillos and soft rolls for the table, the tortas and the week's breakfasts.

The early-morning crowd

Before sunrise is our busiest window. Bakery runs start early on E Tulare Ave, and the first trays prove it.

By the piece or the dozen

Priced like a neighborhood panaderia should be. Pick what you like; the tray adds itself.
