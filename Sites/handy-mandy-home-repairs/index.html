About Handy Mandy

A one woman repair shop with a five star reputation.

Handy Mandy Home Repairs is run by Meagan, known to her customers as Mandy (or Handy Mandy). She is a woman in a trade where that still turns heads, and her customers love her for it: fast, detail oriented, always smiling, and right on time.

Her own words for the business say it best: “Let Handy Mandy tackle that ‘honey-do’ list!” From ceiling fans and fixtures to drywall patches and door fixes, the work is the small, fiddly stuff that keeps a house running and that busy homeowners keep putting off.

The business is based at 2549 Indian Mound Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio, and holds a 5.0 star rating across 9 published reviews. Customers describe fair prices, excellent communication, and work done right the first time.
