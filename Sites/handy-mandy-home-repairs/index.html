About Handy Mandy

A one woman repair shop with a five star reputation.

Handy Mandy Home Repairs is a Cincinnati home-repair business focused on the small household jobs that are easy to put off. Published customer reviews repeatedly describe responsive communication, punctual visits, careful work, and a friendly experience.

Her own words for the business say it best: “Let Handy Mandy tackle that ‘honey-do’ list!” From ceiling fans and fixtures to drywall patches and door fixes, the work is the small, fiddly stuff that keeps a house running and that busy homeowners keep putting off.

The business is based at Cincinnati, Ohio, and holds a 5.0 star rating across 9 published reviews. Customers describe fair prices, excellent communication, and work done right the first time.
