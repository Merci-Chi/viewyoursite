The Shelf

Wines & Spirits

A straightforward downtown selection: the classics you came for, cold when you want it cold.

Wine

Reds, whites, rose and sparkling. Ask the counter if you want a hand picking a bottle for dinner, a gift or the hotel room.

Beer

Packs, imports and craft picks in the cooler. Grab ice on the way out and it is already ready for the evening.

Spirits

Whiskey, tequila, vodka, gin, rum and liqueurs across the price ladder, from well bottles to the good stuff.

Mixers, Ice & Snacks

Soda, juice, tonic, ice and snacks. One register, one stop, back to the River Walk in minutes.

On the tourist corridor
