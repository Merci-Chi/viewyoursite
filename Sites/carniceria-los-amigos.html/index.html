Before you come

Two things to know

Busy on weekend mornings

The counter gets busy early on Saturdays and Sundays. Come in the morning and you will still move fast, but plan for a short line.

Limited parking on 1st St

Street parking around the market is limited. Give yourself a few extra minutes, especially on weekends.
