El Mercado

Mack Road's little market

On Mack Road in south Sacramento, Mi Mercadito packs a carniceria counter and market shelves into a shop your neighbor probably already told you about. The staff is the part people mention first, friendly and always around, and the hot food counter covers the nights nobody wants to cook.

The hot food counter

The counter keeps its own schedule, shorter than the market's. Pickup runs 11 to 7 daily, the hours listed through Grubhub.
