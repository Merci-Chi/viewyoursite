What we can do for you

Whether you need a complete landscape overhaul, a drought-tolerant redesign, or simply someone to keep your yard looking sharp every week — we've got you covered.

Residential Landscaping

We design and build outdoor spaces that complement your home and lifestyle. Every project starts with a conversation about your vision, your budget, and what your yard needs to thrive.

  • Full landscape design & installation
  • Sod, plants, trees & garden beds
  • Hardscaping: patios, walkways, retaining walls
  • Irrigation systems & drainage solutions
  • Outdoor lighting & accent features

Xeriscape Design

Xeriscaping isn't just rocks and cactus — it's a smart, beautiful approach to landscaping that drastically reduces water use while creating a stunning, low-maintenance yard.

  • Drought-tolerant plant selection & placement
  • Smart irrigation with drip systems
  • Decorative gravel, decomposed granite & mulch
  • Soil amendment & water retention strategies
  • Significant water bill savings

A well-maintained yard doesn't happen by accident. Our weekly maintenance program keeps your property looking its best — consistently, reliably, without you having to lift a finger.

  • Lawn mowing, edging & blowing
  • Hedge & shrub trimming
  • Weed control in beds & hardscape
  • Seasonal cleanups & debris removal
  • Fertilization & plant health checks
